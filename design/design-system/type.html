Type — Literata + Noto Serif Georgian

One serif voice set like a book. Literata (Google Fonts) carries Latin and Cyrillic in two weights, 400 and 600 — the whole budget; Noto Serif Georgian is its Georgian companion in the same stack — Literata, "Noto Serif Georgian", Georgia, serif — so Georgian glyphs fall through to a face cut for them. Georgian has no capitals: no role may depend on uppercase to rank, and letterspaced labels track less in Georgian. Russian runs wide: every role below is shown in the language that stresses it most, all three side by side.
