Components at 375px

Every interactive piece keeps a touch area of at least 44×44px (measure by opening this page with ?measure — each measured element is tagged). Frames below are exactly 375px wide; the layout holds without horizontal scroll down to 320px. Webfont budget: Literata 400–600 (one variable file per subset), Noto Serif Georgian 400–600 on the Georgian page only, no italic axis; a language label, a foreign-script menu line and the lari sign fall back to the system stack instead of dragging an alphabet.

The dotted leader hangs from the last line of a wrapped name, so the price never separates from its dish. Names print exactly as the venue’s menu wrote them. Photo card with its one-sentence caption

True aspect ratio, never cropped; a shaded mat and hairline instead of shadows. The caption sits under the photo — text never lies on a photograph — and makes the same claim in all three languages. Every frame ships as a web-sized derivative (79–147 KB) — never an Instagram original.

The arch frame — hero photo (cropped so the hearth leads) and the no-photo fallback

The semicircular top quotes the venue’s round hearth. The photo is cropped so the brick ring and the fire lead the frame and the table setting is incidental. If the photo is withdrawn, the frame holds an authored drawing of the hearth — a mortared brick ring with depth behind it and a sketched flame, its own composition, not the logo rescaled — with the shelf and book stacks around it.
